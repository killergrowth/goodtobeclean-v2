Soda Blasting

Professional Soda Blasting in Kansas

Soda blasting uses food-grade sodium bicarbonate propelled at high pressure to clean and restore surfaces without causing damage to the material underneath. It's the preferred technique for removing mold from wood framing, soot from fire-damaged structures, and old coatings where sandblasting would cause damage.
