What Is Soda Blasting and Why Does It Matter?

Soda blasting is a surface preparation and cleaning method that uses pharmaceutical-grade sodium bicarbonate particles delivered under compressed air pressure. Unlike sandblasting, which is aggressive and can damage soft surfaces, soda blasting is non-destructive -- the bicarbonate particles explode on impact, lifting contaminants without gouging the underlying material. It leaves no hazardous residue and is safe for wood, aluminum, fiberglass, and masonry.

Soda blasting is particularly effective for mold removal on wood framing and structural lumber, fire and smoke restoration on structural surfaces, paint stripping on historic homes and industrial equipment, and rust removal on metal. It removes and decontaminates simultaneously -- unlike chemical treatments that require separate cleaning steps.
