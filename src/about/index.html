About Good To Be Clean

Locally owned. IICRC certified. Serving Kansas since 2014.

Our Story

It's Good To Be Clean!

Good To Be Clean was founded with a simple mission: provide Kansas families and businesses with honest, professional cleaning and restoration services they can truly rely on.

We started in El Dorado, KS and have grown to serve communities across Butler, Greenwood, Sedgwick, and Harvey counties — building a reputation on consistent quality, fair pricing, and work done right the first time.

With over a decade of experience and 200+ five-star reviews, we're proud to be the team our neighbors call when it matters most.
