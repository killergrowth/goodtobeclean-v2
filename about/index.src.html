Our Story

From Humble Beginnings to Full-Service Restoration

Good To Be Clean was built on the foundation of hard work, dedication, and a passion for helping others. In 2012, Good To Be Clean was launched with just a few pieces of equipment and a determination to make a difference.

What started as a small carpet cleaning operation quickly grew as the founders recognized a greater need in their community. Over time, Good To Be Clean expanded into water damage restoration, fire and smoke damage cleanup, and mold remediation — becoming a trusted name for homeowners and businesses facing unexpected disasters.

A Team That Cares

We're the Calm in the Middle of Your Storm

At Good To Be Clean, we understand that when people call us, they're often going through stressful and unexpected situations. Whether it's a flooded basement, fire damage, or mold growth, these challenges can feel overwhelming. That's why we do more than just clean — we help people reclaim their homes and businesses, restoring comfort, safety, and peace of mind.

“We are responsible for being the ‘calm’ in the middle of their storm.”
— Nick Dieker, Managing Partner

Our approach is built on compassion, professionalism, and expertise. We don't just show up to do a job — we advocate for our clients, working with insurance companies and ensuring the process is as smooth as possible.

Leadership

The Leadership Team at Good To Be Clean

Founder & Managing Partner

Samuel McVay

Since the company's inception, Samuel McVay has been instrumental in shaping Good To Be Clean into a trusted name in cleaning and restoration. His dedication to high-quality service and customer satisfaction has helped grow the company into what it is today. Now Samuel focuses on Good To Be Clean's marketing efforts and looks for new opportunities to grow the business.

Managing Partner

Nick Dieker

Nick Dieker joined Good To Be Clean in 2020, but his journey with the company started well before that. Initially working as a carpet cleaner, Nick quickly moved into the restoration side of the business, gaining hands-on experience in water, fire, and mold. His dedication and passion for serving customers led to him becoming a managing partner in 2020, helping guide Good To Be Clean into its next phase of growth.

Our Commitment to Excellence

Over the years, Good To Be Clean has remained dedicated to staying at the forefront of the industry. We invest in ongoing education, earning certifications from IICRC, NADCA, and CSIA, to ensure our team is always equipped with the latest knowledge and best practices.

Our mission is simple…

  • Provide expert cleaning and restoration services with honesty and care.
  • Be a trusted partner in times of need.
  • Continue to learn, grow, and serve our community with excellence.

Good To Be Clean isn't just a business — it's a team of people who care deeply about what they do. And for us, there's nothing more rewarding than helping families and businesses get back on their feet.
