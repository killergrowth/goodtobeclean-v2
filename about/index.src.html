Who We Are

A Kansas Company Built on Trust

Good To Be Clean, LLC is a locally owned cleaning and restoration company serving Wichita, El Dorado, and communities throughout south-central Kansas. We were founded on a simple belief: that every family deserves a clean, safe, and healthy home.

We're not a franchise. We're your neighbors — and we treat every job like we're working in our own home. That means showing up on time, doing the work right the first time, and standing behind our results.

Whether you're dealing with a flooded basement at 2am or just need your carpets freshened up before the holidays, we're here for you.
