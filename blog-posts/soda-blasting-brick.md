# Can You Soda Blast Brick? How It Works and When It's the Right Choice

Soda blasting brick is a proven method for removing paint, organic growth, and mortar residue from masonry surfaces without the surface damage that harsher abrasives can cause. ## What Is Soda Blasting and How Does It Clean Brick?

Soda blasting uses sodium bicarbonate (baking soda) particles propelled at high velocity to clean surfaces. Unlike silica sand or aluminum oxide, sodium bicarbonate is a soft, water-soluble abrasive that fractures on impact. This means it removes contaminants without etching or pitting the underlying brick.

The process works through a combination of kinetic energy and chemical reaction. When the sodium bicarbonate particles hit the brick surface, they lift paint, biological growth, and staining while deodorizing and neutralizing mild acids. This makes brick soda blasting especially effective for removing old oil-based paints, latex coatings, carbon staining, algae, lichen, and mortar smears left behind during masonry work.

Because the medium is softer than the brick itself, soda blast brick cleaning preserves the fired surface of the masonry. This is critical on softer historic brick or on any surface where maintaining the original texture matters. The sodium bicarbonate residue rinses away with water, leaving no embedded abrasive or chemical residue that could cause long-term deterioration.

## When Soda Blasting Is the Right Choice for Brick

Soda blasting works best when you need to remove surface contamination without altering the brick substrate. It's ideal for:

*   **Removing old paint**: Multiple layers of latex or oil-based paint come off cleanly, especially when the paint hasn't fully bonded or is already flaking. This is one of the most common uses for commercial buildings undergoing facade restoration.
*   **Cleaning mortar joints**: Soda blasting removes mortar haze, efflorescence, and staining without eroding joint profiles or damaging repointing work.
*   **Eliminating biological growth**: Mold, mildew, lichen, and algae are lifted from both the brick face and the mortar, particularly in shaded or moisture-prone areas.
*   **Restoring historic masonry**: Because it's non-destructive, soda blasting is often specified for buildings with soft, historic brick that would be damaged by sandblasting or chemical strippers.

Soda blasting is _not_ the best choice when the paint has chemically bonded to the brick surface (common with certain masonry paints applied to raw brick), or when you're dealing with heavy industrial coatings like epoxies or urethanes. In those cases, chemical stripping or a more aggressive abrasive may be needed. ## How Much Does Soda Blasting Brick Cost?

Brick soda blasting cost typically ranges from $3 to $8 per square foot, depending on the scope of the project, the condition of the brick, and the type of contamination being removed. Small residential projectslike cleaning a painted fireplace or a single exterior wallmay fall on the higher end of that range due to setup and mobilization costs. Larger commercial projects, such as cleaning an entire building facade, benefit from economies of scale.

Several factors influence the final cost:

*   **Surface condition**: Heavily painted or stained brick requires more passes and longer cleaning times.
*   **Accessibility**: Multi-story buildings or areas requiring scaffolding or lift equipment add to labor costs.
*   **Containment and cleanup**: Soda blasting generates dust and residue that must be contained, especially in occupied buildings or urban settings.
*   **Rinsing and finishing**: Most projects require a water rinse to remove residue, which adds time and equipment costs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you soda blast brick?

Yes, you can soda blast brick. Soda blasting is a safe and effective method for cleaning brick surfaces because sodium bicarbonate is softer than fired clay, which means it removes contaminants like paint, mold, and mortar residue without etching or pitting the brick itself. It's widely used for both residential and commercial masonry restoration.

### Does soda blasting damage brick?

Soda blasting does not damage brick when performed correctly. The sodium bicarbonate media is non-abrasive relative to the hardness of brick, so it won't erode the surface or mortar joints. However, improper techniquesuch as excessive pressure or prolonged exposure in one areacan cause issues, which is why working with a trained, IICRC-certified technician is important.

### What is the best way to clean brick?

The best way to clean brick depends on what you're removing. For paint, biological growth, and mortar staining, soda blasting is often the most effective and least damaging option. For light dirt and efflorescence, low-pressure washing with a mild detergent may be sufficient. Avoid harsh chemicals or high-pressure sandblasting, which can permanently damage brick and mortar.

### Can soda blasting remove paint from brick?

Yes, soda blasting can remove paint from brick, including multiple layers of latex and oil-based coatings. It's especially effective on soda blasting old paint that's flaking or hasn't fully bonded to the masonry. The process lifts paint residue without damaging the brick surface, making it ideal for restoration work on historic and commercial buildings.

### How much does soda blasting brick cost?

Soda blasting brick typically costs between $3 and $8 per square foot, depending on the project size, surface condition, and accessibility. Smaller residential jobs may cost more per square foot due to setup, while large commercial projects benefit from volume pricing. An on-site assessment provides the most accurate estimate for your specific project.
