## Why Wool Carpets Require Special Handling

Wool is a natural fiber — and that's exactly what makes it tricky. It absorbs moisture readily, which means:

- **Over-wetting causes shrinkage.** Too much water can cause the fibers to contract, distorting the rug permanently.
- **Alkaline cleaners damage the fiber.** Most household carpet cleaners are pH-balanced for synthetic fibers. Wool needs a neutral or slightly acidic cleaner.
- **Bleach is off-limits.** Even diluted bleach will break down wool fibers and strip color.
- **Heat can set stains and cause yellowing.** High-temperature steam applied improperly can yellow wool.

For Wichita homeowners with wool area rugs or wall-to-wall wool carpet, the safest approach is professional cleaning by someone experienced with natural fibers — ideally using low-moisture or controlled hot water extraction with pH-balanced solutions.

## Berber Carpet: Durable, But Easy to Damage

Berber carpet has a distinctive looped construction that gives it its signature texture and durability. That same construction is what makes it vulnerable during cleaning.

**Common mistakes with Berber:**

- **Aggressive scrubbing snags and unravels the loops.** Once a loop pulls, it can run like a stocking — across a large section of your floor.
- **Rotating brush attachments cause fuzz.** Many consumer carpet cleaners use rotating heads that are fine for cut-pile carpets but damaging to looped construction.
- **Liquid pooling in the dense weave.** Berber holds moisture deep in the pile, which can lead to mold or mildew underneath if not dried properly.

If your Berber is made from olefin (the most common material), it resists stains well but attracts oil — so greasy spots need a solvent-based approach, not just hot water.

## What to Do Between Professional Cleanings

For day-to-day maintenance on both wool and Berber carpets:

1. **Vacuum regularly, but gently.** Use a suction-only setting or a vacuum without a beater bar, especially on Berber.
2. **Blot — don't rub — spills immediately.** Work from the outside of the stain inward to avoid spreading.
3. **Test any cleaner in a hidden area first.** This goes double for wool.
4. **Keep foot traffic manageable.** Use area rugs in high-traffic zones to protect your investment.

## When to Call a Professional in Wichita

Some situations are best left to the pros:

- Annual deep cleaning (especially in higher-traffic homes or homes with pets)
- After a significant spill, especially anything oily, colored, or acidic
- Any time you notice odors — moisture trapped in the backing can cause mold growth
- If the carpet has been flooded or experienced water damage
