# How Much Does Carpet Cleaning Cost? A Pricing Guide for Homeowners

If you're searching for the **cost for carpet cleaning**, you're probably trying to budget for your next deep cleanor deciding whether a quote you received is fair. The answer isn't one-size-fits-all, but most homeowners in south-central Kansas can expect to pay between $25 and $75 per room, or $0.20 to $0.50 per square foot, depending on the method used and the condition of the carpets. ## What Determines Carpet Cleaning Prices?

The **carpet cleaning price** you pay depends on several factors: the size of the area, the cleaning method, carpet condition, and whether you're dealing with stains, pet odors, or high-traffic wear.

Most companies charge either **per room** or **per square foot**. Per-room pricing typically ranges from $25 to $75 per room, with a minimum service charge (often $100$150 total). Per-square-foot pricing is more precise and usually runs $0.20 to $0.50 per square foot. For a typical 1,500-square-foot home, expect to pay $200 to $400 for a full cleaning.

Carpet condition matters. Heavily soiled carpets, stubborn stains, or areas with pet urine often require pretreatment, stain-specific solutions, or enzyme treatmentseach of which adds to the cost. If your carpets haven't been cleaned in years, expect pricing to reflect the extra labor and chemicals needed.

The cleaning method also drives price. IICRC-certified hot water extraction (steam cleaning) costs more than dry cleaning or bonnet cleaning, but it's the only method recommended by most carpet manufacturers. Hot water extraction reaches deep into carpet fibers, removing dirt, allergens, and bacteria that surface-level methods miss. Cheaper methods may look good initially, but they don't extend carpet life the way proper extraction does.

## IICRC Certification and Why It Affects Cost

When you hire a company that's IICRC certified, like Good To Be Clean, you're paying for technicians trained to industry standards. The Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification sets the gold standard for carpet care, and certified techs know how to match the right cleaning agents and equipment to your carpet type.

**How much to clean carpet** professionally using IICRC methods might be $50$100 more than a budget service, but you're getting proper extraction, faster dry times, and protection against mold or resoiling. Non-certified cleaners may oversaturate carpets, use harsh chemicals that void warranties, or skip steps that prevent mildew growth.

Professional equipment also costs more to operate. Truck-mounted steam cleaning systemsstandard in IICRC workprovide more suction and higher heat than portable units. That means cleaner carpets and shorter drying times (usually 612 hours instead of 24+). If a company quotes significantly below market rate, ask what equipment and training they're using. You may be paying less upfront but risking damage or poor results.

Quality companies also carry proper insurance and bonding. If something goes wrongwater damage, discoloration, or injuryyou're covered. Unlicensed operators offering rock-bottom prices rarely have that protection.

## How to Compare Quotes and Avoid Pricing Traps

Start by asking whether pricing is per room or per square foot, and what constitutes a "room." Some companies cap room size at 200 square feet, charging extra for larger spaces. Others exclude hallways, closets, or stairs from base pricing.

Watch out for **bait-and-switch pricing**. Ads promising "$99 whole house cleaning" often come with asterisks: only certain rooms, limited square footage, or add-on fees for pretreatment, furniture moving, and deodorizingthings most homeowners assume are included. A legitimate company will provide a transparent estimate upfront.

Ask what's included in the **professional carpet cleaning cost**. Standard service should cover vacuuming, pretreatment of high-traffic areas, hot water extraction, and grooming. Extras like stain protection, pet odor removal, or Scotchgard application cost more, but they should be clearly itemized.

Request references or online reviews. A company offering [carpet cleaning services](/carpet-cleaning/) should have a track record you can verify. If pricing seems too good to be true and there's no digital footprint, that's a red flag.

Finally, get quotes from at least two companies. Comparing estimates helps you understand the local market and identify outliers. If you're located in Wichita, where the median home size is around 1,400 square feet, expect a full-home cleaning to run $175 to $350 depending on carpet condition and method. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much does carpet cleaning cost per room?

Carpet cleaning typically costs $25 to $75 per room, depending on room size, carpet condition, and cleaning method. Most companies set a minimum charge of $100 to $150 for service calls. Per-room pricing works best for smaller homes, but per-square-foot pricing is often more accurate for large or irregularly shaped spaces. Expect higher costs for rooms with heavy staining or pet odors.

### Is professional carpet cleaning worth it?

Yes. Professional carpet cleaning extends carpet life, improves indoor air quality, and removes deep-set dirt and allergens that vacuuming can't reach. IICRC-certified hot water extraction is the only method recommended by most carpet manufacturers and is often required to maintain warranty coverage. DIY rental machines lack the suction power and heat of professional equipment, leading to longer dry times and less effective cleaning.

### How often should you professionally clean carpets?

Most carpet manufacturers recommend professional cleaning every 12 to 18 months. High-traffic homes, homes with pets, or households with allergies should clean carpets every 6 to 12 months. Regular professional cleaning prevents soil buildup, reduces allergens, and keeps carpets looking newer longer. Waiting too long between cleanings can allow dirt to damage carpet fibers, shortening their lifespan.

### What is the average cost for carpet cleaning?

The average cost for professional carpet cleaning ranges from $200 to $400 for a typical 1,500-square-foot home. Per-square-foot pricing averages $0.20 to $0.50, while per-room pricing runs $25 to $75 per room. Costs vary based on carpet condition, the cleaning method used, and regional labor rates. Add-ons like stain protection, pet treatments, or furniture moving increase the total price.

### How do I get the best price on carpet cleaning?

Request quotes from multiple certified companies and compare what's included. Ask whether pricing is per room or per square foot, and confirm what counts as a "room." Avoid bait-and-switch ads with unrealistic pricing. Schedule service during off-peak seasons (late fall or winter) when demand is lower. Some companies offer discounts for first-time customers or multi-service bookings, such as combining carpet and upholstery cleaning.
