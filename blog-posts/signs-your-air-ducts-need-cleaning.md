## How Do You Know When It's Time?

Most Wichita homeowners don't think about their air ducts until something goes wrong — and by then, the problem has usually been building for a while. Your duct system quietly moves air through every room in your house, but it also collects dust, pet dander, pollen, mold spores, and debris over time.

The EPA generally recommends having your air ducts inspected regularly, and cleaning them when there's clear evidence of contamination. But what does that actually look like? Here are the signs worth paying attention to.

---

## 7 Signs Your Air Ducts Need Cleaning

### 1. Dust Blowing Out of Your Vents

If you see a visible puff of dust when your HVAC kicks on — or you notice dust settling on furniture shortly after cleaning — that's a direct signal. Your ducts may be circulating built-up debris throughout your home every time the system runs.

### 2. Visible Mold Around Vents or Inside Ducts

Mold in or around your ductwork is one of the most urgent signs that action is needed. Kansas humidity, especially during spring and summer, creates the right conditions for mold to take hold inside your HVAC system. If you see dark spots around vent covers or notice a musty odor when the air runs, don't wait — that's potentially a health issue, not just a cleaning one.

Our [mold removal services](/service/mold-removal/) cover situations where mold has spread beyond just the vents.

### 3. Unexplained Spike in Energy Bills

If your heating or cooling costs have crept up without a clear reason, clogged ducts might be the culprit. When airflow is restricted by buildup, your HVAC has to work harder to maintain temperature — which means it runs longer and burns more energy.

### 4. Inconsistent Airflow Room to Room

Walk through your house and hold your hand near each vent. If some rooms feel noticeably weaker than others, blockages inside your duct system may be restricting flow. This is especially common in older Wichita homes where ductwork hasn't been serviced in years.

### 5. Allergy or Respiratory Symptoms That Won't Quit

Wichita already has rough allergy seasons — the last thing you need is your own home making it worse. If household members are experiencing persistent sneezing, coughing, itchy eyes, or aggravated asthma symptoms that seem to ease up when they leave the house, your indoor air quality may be the issue. Ducts full of dust, pet dander, and allergens recirculate those particles every time your system runs.

Our [air duct cleaning service](/service/air-duct-cleaning/) is specifically designed to clear out what's been building up and improve the air your family breathes.

### 6. You've Recently Finished a Renovation

Drywall dust and construction debris are notorious for infiltrating duct systems during remodeling work. Even with precautions, fine particles find their way in. If you've had any work done — new flooring, a kitchen remodel, an addition — a duct cleaning after the project wraps is almost always a good call.

### 7. You Can't Remember the Last Time They Were Cleaned

This one's simple. If you've lived in your home for several years and duct cleaning has never come up, it's probably overdue. Most homes benefit from a professional cleaning every 3–5 years, more often if you have pets, smokers, or family members with respiratory conditions.

---

## What Happens If You Ignore It?

Dirty ducts don't just stay dirty — they get worse. Buildup restricts airflow, strains your HVAC system, reduces its lifespan, and keeps circulating contaminants through your home. In worst-case scenarios, moisture in the ducts leads to mold growth, which turns a simple cleaning into a full [restoration situation](/service/restoration-services/).

Catching it early is almost always cheaper and less disruptive.
